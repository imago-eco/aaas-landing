ALPHA · INVITE ONLY · 2026

IMA.AAAS

Your own persistent AI orchestrator and a small specialist team, running 24/7 on Even's home server.

Not a SaaS. Not a chatbot. A small standing AI team that gets to know you and helps with your stuff.

WHO IT'S FOR

Five friends Even picked. That's the whole alpha.

If you're reading this, you're one of them — or someone Even sent the link to.

We kept it small on purpose. We want to know whether a persistent AI team is genuinely useful or whether the magic wears off in week two, and five lets us hear every signal.

If it works, it grows. If it doesn't, you'll get the unvarnished post-mortem and we'll all have learned something.

WHAT YOU GET

A team of three, named once you meet them.

ROLE · 01

YOUR ORCHESTRATOR

🐙 your orchestrator

When you join, the first message you get is "what should I call myself?" — pick a name, it'll answer to it from then on. It remembers what you've told it, what you're working on, what you care about. When a job needs a researcher or a builder, it pulls one in. Asks before doing anything irreversible.

ROLE · 02

A RESEARCHER ON CALL

🐿️ pulled in when needed

When the orchestrator needs facts, it pulls in a researcher. Reads sources and cites them. Says "I'm not sure" when it isn't. Quality-scores its own work before handing back. Verdict first, then the reasoning — no padding.

ROLE · 03

A BUILDER ON CALL

🦀 pulled in when needed

When the orchestrator needs something made — code, a script, a doc, a small tool — the builder picks it up. Skeptical by design: it'll say "this is a bad idea, here's why" before implementing. Tests its work. Doesn't ship broken things.

HOW IT WORKS

01

Even sends you an invite link

One Discord server, your own private channel. Your orchestrator joins it and posts first.

02

You name your orchestrator

It asks. You pick. From that point it answers to that name and remembers everything you tell it. Memory is yours — pause or delete anytime by telling Even.

03

You ask it to do stuff

Research, drafting, brainstorming, scripts, finding things, summarizing things. It'll say what it can and can't do. If it needs to do something irreversible, it'll ask first.

04

GitHub for the longer-running stuff

If you've got work you want tracked, your orchestrator plugs into a GitHub repo. Issues become tasks. You can also ignore this entirely and just use Discord — it works either way.

WHAT IT ISN'T

  • Not a SaaS. No login, no account, no billing. You're a guest in Even's setup.
  • No SLA. It runs when Even's box runs. If his power blips, your team blips. He'll tell you.
  • Alpha-stage. Things will break. We'll be honest when they do.
  • Runs on Even's hardware. Your conversations sit on his Dell in Oslo.
  • Pause anytime. Tell Even. The kill-switch is one command. No friction.
